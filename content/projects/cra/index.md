CRA processes vendor data dumps, segments potential clients, and ranks high-value opportunities using project fit and revenue potential.

The solution helps sales teams focus on the most promising prospects and supports data-informed consulting and pre-sales decisions.

**Technical focus:** Python, Pandas, SQL, machine learning, Power BI, segmentation, and opportunity scoring.

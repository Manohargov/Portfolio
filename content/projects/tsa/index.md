TSA extracts dimensions and tolerances from engineering PDFs, performs tolerance stack-up analysis, and automatically produces structured reports.

The workflow reduces repetitive manual engineering effort while improving the traceability of source dimensions, calculations, and reported results.

**Technical focus:** Python, GPT API, OCR, PDF processing, engineering calculations, and automated reporting.
